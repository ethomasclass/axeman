Play jazz, or get the axe.

In March 1919, a letter signed by a serial killer made that offer to the whole city of New Orleans.

For almost a year, someone had been chiseling through back doors at night and attacking people in their own homes.

Then this letter ran in the Times-Picayune. It was dated from Hell. And it was signed: the Axeman.

At 12:15 on Tuesday night, he wrote, he would pass over the city. Every home with a jazz band in full swing would be spared. Anyone who didn't jazz it out would get the axe.

So New Orleans jazzed it out. Dance halls packed. Bands at house parties all over town.

12:15 came and went.

*Nobody was attacked that night.*

The letter was almost certainly a prank. And the Axeman? *He was never caught.*
