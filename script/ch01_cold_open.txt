A piece of sheet music. New Orleans, 1919.

On the cover, a whole family is playing as loud as they can. Piano. Trombone. A kid on a drum. And the door is wide open behind them.

The title: The Mysterious Axman's Jazz. And underneath, in smaller letters: Don't Scare Me, Papa.

That's a song about a serial killer. And people bought it.

Because on one night in March 1919, *almost every dance hall in New Orleans was packed*. Bands played in living rooms all over the city, until well after midnight. The city wasn't celebrating. A killer had told them to.

So here's the question I want to answer today: *How did a killer get a whole city to throw him a party?*

It starts in a corner grocery, with a hole in the back door.
