But whoever the Axeman was, he wasn't finished.

August 10th, 1919. A grocer named Steve Boca wakes up to a figure standing over his bed. He survives, with no memory of the attack. A panel has been chiseled out of his back door.

September 3rd. Sarah Laumann, 19 and living alone, is found unconscious in her bed. She survives too. A bloody axe lies on the lawn.

October 27th. Mike Pepitone, a grocer and a father of six. His wife hears a noise and sees a large man run from the room. Pepitone dies of his wounds.

That's the last attack anyone has tied to the Axeman.

He doesn't get caught. He doesn't write again. *He just stops.*
