## Notes on sources and licences

- **Commons clippings** (Maggio, Besumer, Davi, Rissetto, Crutti, Sciambra, Andollina, the cartoon, the two maps, the sheet music, Manfre/LA Times): downloaded at their full native size. Most are small (220–670 px) because the Commons uploads are small. They read fine as half-screen "clipping" cards with a slow push-in, but don't run them full screen.
- **Sheet music**: the cover is on Commons (`File:Mysterious axman.gif`, 1919, PD), so it came from Commons and no HNOC permission is needed for this copy. HNOC holds the original if a higher-res scan is ever wanted. `ch01/mysterious_axman_jazz_cover_1919_display.jpg` was already in the folder before this pass and has no credits entry of its own; it uses the same credit as the `.jpg` next to it.
- **Chronicling America crops** were cut at native resolution from the LoC IIIF image service. The credits entries give the page URL and the exact IIIF region URL. Licence: US newspapers published before 1931.
- **Genthe photos** (LoC, c. 1920–26) and **Detroit Publishing Co.** photos: LoC says "No known restrictions". They were TIFFs, converted to 3000 px JPEGs.
- **Commons access:** upload.wikimedia.org returned HTTP 429 for this machine's IP for the whole session. The Commons files were fetched through Commons' own `w/thumb.php` endpoint at native or capped size, and that endpoint also started returning 429 near the end. Credits still use the `commons.get` format.
- **Map:** `maps/times_picayune_map_new_orleans_1919.jpg` is 6000×5042 px, about 6 MB. Source: Boston Public Library / Leventhal Map Center via Digital Commonwealth IIIF; the full original is 9073×7624. It shows the whole city with streets and streetcar lines, and street names are legible at 6000 px.

## Gaps (candidates for the few AI paintings, or for a later retry)

1. **The Axeman letter itself as printed in the Times-Picayune (14 Mar 1919):** not on Commons or Chronicling America, because the Times-Picayune isn't digitized there. Best substitute: `ch07/that_axmans_letter_herald_nola_1919-03-20.jpg`, which describes it. Could also typeset the letter text over a period-paper texture instead of using AI.
2. **Cortimiglia / Romano / Schneider / Boca / Laumann portraits or clippings:** no PD images found. Cortimiglia and Jordano are covered by 3 AP text clippings in ch06; Pepitone by an AP item in ch08. No images of the Aug 1918 attacks (Schneider, Romano) or the Aug–Sep 1919 attacks (Boca, Laumann).
3. **Gretna, c. 1919:** no period photo of Gretna. Stand-ins are the Mississippi transfer boat (1905), the levee at the foot of Canal St and the courtroom photos. A Gretna street or ferry landing at night is a good AI-painting candidate.

5. **Period axe / hatchet, straight razor, shotgun-house back door (ch02):** not gathered yet. Plan was IA catalogue pages, e.g. `python3 tools/find_images.py books "Sears Roebuck catalogue"` then `pages`.

## What the video uses (added after the build)

- **Every picture's role and on-screen tag** is in `src/pics.ts`; sizes in `src/imgs.ts`.
- **The cover display copy** (`ch01/mysterious_axman_jazz_cover_1919_display.jpg`): identical to the Commons file except
  one line of small print (the title of Davilla's earlier song, which contains a racial slur) painted out with paper
  colour. Same credit as the original, which stays unaltered beside it.
- **Masks (coral tint + teal trace):** photographs cut out with rembg (`tools/mask.py`): `market_cart`,
  `flu_patient`, `ferry`. Clippings and drawings traced by hand (`tools/polymask.py`): `sheet_piano`, `besumer`,
  `cartoon_guard`, `maggios`.
- **Parallax (2.5D) layers** (`tools/layers.py` → `public/img/layers/`): the French Market cart (ch05), the flu ward
  (ch04) and the Gretna ferry (ch06).
- **Not used:** the Morgan City front page (no Axeman story on it), the 1891 *Judge* cartoon (a caricature), the
  army jazz band at Camp Upton (not New Orleans), the Seattle flu photo, the Wayne County courtroom is used as "a
  courtroom" only.
